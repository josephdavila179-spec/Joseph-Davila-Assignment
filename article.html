City Council Approves Major Downtown Park Revamp

By Joseph Davila

After months of community feedback and city planning meetings, the city council officially voted unanimously last night to fund a multi-million dollar overhaul of the downtown central park.

Construction is scheduled to break ground early next spring. Key additions to the project include expanded walking and biking paths, energy-efficient street lighting, modernized playground equipment, and updated seating areas designed to make the space safer and more accessible for community events.

City officials expect the first phase of construction to be completed by late summer. Local business owners have expressed strong support for the project, hoping the improved public space will bring more foot traffic to neighboring shops and restaurants.
